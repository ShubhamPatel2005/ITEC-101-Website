My personal values

  • Keeping Family Close

    • I am very close to my family, and they mean a lot to me.
    They've done a lot for me and that's why I keep them close.
    It is important to me because they've never left my side.
    If I were to not adhere to this, I feel I would lose a big part of myself.

  • Being disciplined

    • I like to try to remain disciplined because I like to envision a big life for myself in the future.
    Not only that, but I've worked pretty hard to get to where I am, so why ruin it by not
    remaining disciplined. It's important to me for those reasons. If I don't stay disciplined
    I could become a slacker and not live up to the person I want to become, which would be a nightmare.

  • Respecting people

    • I always knew and it was engraved into my mind that I should treat people
    how I would want to be treated. It means a lot to me especially because of how I've seen
    people be treated when I was younger. This also derives from my parents. It's important because
    it goes a long way to be nice. I wouldn't know how far the consequences could go if I didn't adhere to this.

  • Taking care of my body

    I know your body is a machine, and if you take good care of it, when you're older you
    won’t have to worry about your body letting you down. I go to the gym, walk a lot, (try to) eat healthy
    and from listening to older people I learned these are the most important aspects of taking care of our bodies.
    The importance is signified and shows itself when we grow older. If I don't adhere to this my body could
    become sluggish, and I would have all sorts of pains and body issues once I get older.
