Industry Information

  • Describing the industry

    • The banking industry is made up of a multitude of institutions that help manage clients money.
    More specifically, they handle deposits, withdrawals, loans and provide clients with top
    of the line financial advisory. The banking indsutry keeps the economy lively, by managing the flow of money.

  • My role in the industry

    • My role in the industry is as a software developer. My main focus is building and maintaining our banking
    apps, databases, and being able to handle any and all bugs that arise revolving around the softwares
    being developed. So for example, I could develop an app that shows customers their bank balance,
    transactions, statements and anything of the sort that a normal banking app would do.

  • Potential ethical issue

    • The biggest ethical dilemma that arises in the midst of software developers in the banking industry
    is how the sensitive data of clients is handled. For example, even a software developer just letting
    their curiosity get the best of them and looking at sensitive customer information like their statements
    or transactions is still a breach of trust that shouldn't be taken lightly.

  • Two Cybersecurity Issues

    The biggest cybersecurity issue I would say is data breaches. The data breaches can happen when malicious hackers
    break into a banking system and steal sensitive client information like login credentials or any customer information.
    The second biggest cybersecurity I would say is a combination of phishing and social engineering. Both of these go hand and hand
    that work by tricking employees or clients into clicking links in emails, or by posing as other important figures in the company
    to steal money and financial information.

  • Best Cybersecurity Practices

    Although there are many practices for cybersecurity I'd like to highlight two of them. These two being having storng encryption
    around all data and incorporating multi-factor authentication. Encrypted data makes it so that even if data is stolen it is rendered
    completely useless. It makes it impossible to read and decrypt because the keys to decrypting are stored separately and changed
    frequently. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is a method of "double" protecting accounts by not only requiring a password, but
    an extra step of verification like a code from an email, or some sort of facial recognition. This way, even if a hacker gets ahold
    of login credentials, there is that extra step of verification that they can't penetrate, reducing the number of accounts being hacked.
